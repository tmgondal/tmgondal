# Hi, I'm Taimoor Muzaffar Gondal

## Electrical Engineer | PhD Candidate | CSIRO Postgraduate Researcher | R&D Engineer | AI Practitioner

I am an Electrical Engineer and PhD candidate at **Edith Cowan University, Western Australia**, working on advanced control, stability, and intelligent monitoring of modern power and energy systems. My work combines **power electronics, smart grids, battery energy storage, grid-connected inverters, machine learning, and real-time monitoring systems**.

I am currently involved in research and development projects with **Edith Cowan University, CSIRO, Magellan Power, and iMOVE CRC**, focusing on electric vehicle battery repurposing, energy storage systems, under-frequency load-shedding prevention, remote-area EV charging infrastructure, and advanced inverter control for unbalanced distribution networks.
